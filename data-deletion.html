GetHelpApp — Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

GetHelpApp respects your right to control your personal data. If you would like to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Data Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to our support address (below) from the email address associated with your GetHelpApp account.

Step 2: Include the subject line: "Data Deletion Request"

Step 3: In the email body, include your full name and the phone number registered on your account, so we can verify your identity.

Step 4: We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation once your data has been deleted.

Email: inbakumarrajagopal@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we will delete the following data associated with your account:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and date of birth
  • Blood group, height, and weight information
  • Your account password (managed securely via Firebase Authentication)
  • Any voice notes you have submitted
  • For Official accounts: badge number, department affiliation, and submitted ID verification photos

What Data May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for a limited period even after a deletion request, for the following reasons:

  • Case history records (past emergency requests, locations, and outcomes) may be retained in de-identified or aggregated form for up to 90 days, to support ongoing safety investigations, dispute resolution, or legal compliance obligations related to emergency service coordination.
  • Backup systems — data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being permanently purged, as part of our standard data retention and disaster recovery process.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process or about how your data is handled, please contact us at inbakumarrajagopal@gmail.com.
