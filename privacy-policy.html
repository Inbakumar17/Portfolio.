Privacy Policy for GetHelpApp

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

Introduction

GetHelpApp ("we," "our," or "the App") is an emergency alert application that connects citizens with Fire, Health/Ambulance, and Police department officials in real time. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using GetHelpApp, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

For All Users (Citizens and Officials)

  • Name — for identification during emergency response
  • Email address — for account creation and login
  • Phone number — for emergency contact and account verification
  • Date of birth — for identity verification
  • Blood group, height, and weight — shared with responding officials during an emergency to assist with medical response
  • Password — securely stored and encrypted via Firebase Authentication; we never store your password in plain text

Location Information

  • Precise GPS location is collected when you send an emergency alert, so responding officials can locate you
  • Live location tracking is used during an active emergency case, shared between the requester and the responding official until the case is marked complete or cancelled
  • Location is not tracked continuously in the background outside of active emergency requests

Voice Notes

  • If you choose to record a voice note when sending an alert, the audio is transmitted to the responding official to provide additional context about your emergency
  • Voice notes are stored securely and are only accessible to the assigned responding official

Official-Specific Information

  • Badge/Employee ID number and department affiliation
  • Government-issued ID photo, submitted for identity verification before an official account is approved to respond to emergencies

Push Notification Data

  • We collect a device push token (via Expo/Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver real-time alerts, status updates, and case confirmations, even when the app is closed

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Connect emergency requesters with the nearest available, verified official
  • Provide real-time status updates on emergency requests (accepted, en route, arrived, completed)
  • Verify the identity of officials before granting them access to respond to emergencies
  • Send push notifications relevant to your active emergency case or official duty status
  • Maintain a case history for accountability and reporting purposes
  • Notify nearby users when an emergency vehicle is approaching their location ("route clearance" notifications)

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore Database and Firebase Authentication), a secure cloud infrastructure provider. We implement industry-standard security practices, including:

  • Encrypted password storage
  • Role-based access control (citizens, officials, and administrators have different data access permissions)
  • Firestore security rules restricting who can read or modify specific data

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing

Your information is shared only as follows:

  • With responding officials, during an active emergency: your name, phone number, blood group, height, weight, location, and any voice note or text comment you provide
  • With administrators, for the purpose of verifying official accounts and monitoring platform activity for safety and quality assurance
  • We do not share your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers

Data Retention

  • Case history (including past emergency requests, locations, and outcomes) is retained to support accountability, official performance review, and dispute resolution
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us using the information below, subject to any legal or safety-related retention requirements

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for location or notification permissions at any time via your device settings (note: this may limit the App's core functionality)

Children's Privacy

GetHelpApp is not intended for use by children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Regional Availability

GetHelpApp is currently available only to users located in the United States and India. We verify your device's location to confirm eligibility before providing access to emergency services features.

Third-Party Services

GetHelpApp uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database) — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Maps Platform (location and mapping services) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Expo / Expo Push Notifications — https://expo.dev/privacy
